About me

My work brings together hands-on hardware research and a growing focus on algorithms for intelligent robots. I earned an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Northeastern University and a B.Eng. in Mechatronics Engineering from the Federal University of Technology Owerri, Nigeria. I enjoy solving technical problems across simulation, experimentation, and software.
