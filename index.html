My research interests span robot learning, planning, control, and decision-making under uncertainty. Broadly, I am interested in balancing safety and efficiency in autonomous robots operating in dynamic, real-world environments. I am particularly interested in developing learning-based planning and control methods that enable robots to adapt to changing conditions, make informed decisions with limited data or prior experience, reason about the intentions of humans and other robots, and recognize when communication or additional information is necessary for safe coordination. I am also interested in exploring how foundation models, such as large language models (LLMs), can be integrated with learning-based and model-based planning and control methods to help robots better understand complex tasks, reason about their goals, and translate high-level instructions into safe and effective actions.

ROS 2 GNSS Device Driver and Positioning Error Analysis

A ROS 2 driver for live GNSS data, with analysis of positioning accuracy.

  • ROS 2
  • Python
  • GNSS

Parsed NMEA GPGGA messages, published custom ROS 2 messages, and converted coordinates to UTM. Recorded stationary and walking data with ROS 2 bag and evaluated positioning error using standard deviation, 2DRMS, and trajectory analysis.

Deep Reinforcement Learning for Stochastic Maze Navigation

Comparing deep reinforcement learning agents in an uncertain maze environment.

  • PyTorch
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • DQN

Built a stochastic 8×8 maze and compared DQN, Double DQN, and Dueling DQN agents. Evaluated learning stability, exploration, and navigation policies through convergence, trajectory, and learning-rate analysis.

Motion Planning for Ackermann Vehicles using Dubins-Constrained RRT* Variants

Comparing feasible motion planners for Ackermann-steered autonomous vehicles.

  • Motion Planning
  • RRT*
  • Dubins Paths

Evaluated Dubins-constrained RRT*, Bi-RRT*, and P-RRT* across more than 300 simulation trials. Compared planning speed, path length, and success rates; Bi-RRT* achieved approximately 98% success across the tested environments.

Papers

Selected peer-reviewed publications

IEEE Sensors Letters · 2026 First Author

Coil Geometry Effects on Force Directionality and Crosstalk in Direct Ink Writing-Based Electromagnetic Actuators for E-skin

Chiamaka G. Akah, Ravinder Dahiya

Investigated the influence of coil geometry on force directionality, magnetic field distribution, and crosstalk in electromagnetic actuators for robotic electronic skin. Combined finite element simulations with experimental validation of direct ink writing-fabricated spiral coils to establish geometry-based design guidelines for flexible actuator arrays.

Advanced Materials · 2026 Review Article Second Author

Soft Skins With Reversible Thickness Morphing: Materials, Mechanisms, and Applications

Oliver Ozioko, Chiamaka Akah, Ravinder Dahiya

Reviewed recent advances in soft skins capable of reversible out-of-plane deformation, covering actuation mechanisms, material systems, fabrication techniques, and emerging applications. Discussed key challenges and future directions toward intelligent, adaptive soft skins for robotics and haptic interfaces.
