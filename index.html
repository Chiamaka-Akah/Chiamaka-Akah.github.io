About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at Northeastern University. I also hold an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Northeastern University and a B.Eng. in Mechatronics Engineering from the Federal University of Technology Owerri.

My research interests lie in robotics, particularly developing intelligent robots that can learn, reason, and adapt to uncertain and dynamic environments.

Research Interests

Robot Learning, Reinforcement Learning, Motion Planning, Robot Perception, and Decision-Making Under Uncertainty.
